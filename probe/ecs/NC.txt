Source
UNC Policy 400.1.5
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Comprehensive Articulation Agreement allows for seamless transfer between two- and four-year public institutions. The Universal General Education Transfer Component comprises a minimum of 30 semester hours of credit. These are fully transferable to all two and four-year public institutions. There is an additional general education, pre-major and elective courses which bring the total transfer hours to 60-61.
Source
Comprehensive Articulation Agreement Board Policy

Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Students who graduate with a two-year associate of arts or associate of science degree will have fulfilled the UNC institution's lower division of general education credits. Students must have an overall GPA of at least 2.0 and must meet all application requirements at the receiving institution. When students transfer they are guaranteed junior standing.
Source
Comprehensive Articulation Agreement Board Policy
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
However there is a collaborative effort between North Carolina's Community Colleges and the University of North Carolina's 16 institutions. Students must have completed 16 college-level credits at a community college that can apply toward a degree. All 16 or more credits must be earned at one community college. Must earn a total of 60 credits between the institutions that meet associate of arts degree requirements.
