88:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
88:The | 108:Common Data Set (CDS) | 225:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher
88:education community and publishers as represented by the | 347:College Board | 414:, | 419:Peterson’s | 469:, and | 494:U.S.
88:News & World Report | 188:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and
88:accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as
88:well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
88:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set
88:of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the
88:participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to
88:each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of
88:items from the CDS.
88:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts
88:specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when
88:responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their
88:answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
