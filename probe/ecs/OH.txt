State Profile - Ohio

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, has a universal course equivalency classification system for state institutions of higher education.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Ohio Transfer 36, previously called Ohio Transfer Module, is a subset or complete set of an institution's general education requirements in AA, AS or BA degrees. Institutions can have general education requirements that go beyond the Ohio Transfer 36. The Ohio Transfer 36 contains 36-40 semester hours in English, Math, Arts and humanities, social and behavioral sciences, and natural sciences. Students who completed the Ohio Transfer 36 at the sending institution will have met the Ohio Transfer 36 requirements of the receiving institution.
Source
Board Policy D.2.a
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Any associate degree offered at a state institution may be transferred and applied to a bachelor degree program in an equivalent field at any other state institution without unnecessary duplication or institutional barriers.
Source
Ohio Rev. Code § 3333.16
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Credit When It's Due provides opportunity for students with prior college credit to be awarded an associate degree or certificate by applying credit earned before and/or after they began enrollment in a degree or certificate program. Participating institutions collaborate to exchange the academic records of eligible students with the associate degree-granting institution.
Source
Board Policy D.2.k.
