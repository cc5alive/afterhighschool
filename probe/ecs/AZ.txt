Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Arizona

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The community college districts and the universities under the jurisdiction of the Arizona board of regents shall develop and implement a shared numbering system which identifies courses that transfer from community colleges to Arizona public universities toward a baccalaureate degree.
Source
Ariz. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 15-1824
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Community college districts and universities must cooperate in operating a statewide articulation and transfer system including the process for transfer of lower division general education credits.
Source
Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 15-1824
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however AZ Transfer pathways offers guaranteed transfer of some associate degrees, but it is not statewide
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
