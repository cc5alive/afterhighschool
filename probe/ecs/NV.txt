State Profile - Nevada

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
All undergraduate courses in the NSHE must be common-course numbered with equivalent courses offered throughout the system.
Source
Board of Regents Title 4, Chapter 14, Section 17
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The completion of the Associate of Arts, Associate of Science and Associate of Business degree at a community college automatically fulfills the lower-division general education requirements at any other NSHE institution.
Source
Board of Regents Title 4, Chapter 14, Section 16 and Nev. Rev. Stat. 396.568
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The completion of the associate of arts, associate of science and associate of business degree at a community college automatically fulfills the lower-division general education requirements at any other NSHE institution. Nev. Rev. Stat. 396.568 states that a student who completes an associate degree is deemed to have completed the course of study required of a sophomore and should enter at junior status.
Source
Board of Regents Title 4, Chapter 14, Section 16 and Nev. Rev. Stat. § 396.568
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is a degree-completion opportunity for students who started at a community college and transferred to a four-year NSHE institution before completion of an associate degree. Course credits may be transferred from the university or college to the community college to meet the requirements of an associate degree.
Source
Board of Regents Title 4, Chapter 14, Section 19
