Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Arkansas

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The Arkansas Higher Education Coordinating Board, in collaboration with all state-supported institutions of higher education, shall implement a statewide common course numbering system that: 1) Provides improved program planning; 2) Increases communication among all delivery systems; 3) Facilitates the transfer of students and credits between state-supported institutions of higher education; and 4) Promotes consistency in course designation and identification.
Source
Ark. Code Ann. § 6-61-1401
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Requires the Arkansas Higher Education Coordinating Board to establish a minimum core of courses which apply toward the general education core curriculum requirements for associate and baccalaureate degrees at state-supported institutions which are fully transferable between all state-supported institutions of higher education.
Source
Ark. Code Ann. § 6-61-218
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Creates the statewide transfer agreement. State minimum core curriculum means 60 semester hours of lower-division coursework that satisfy the requirements of an associate of arts/science/teaching and selected baccalaureate degrees at all public institutions. Requires a four-year public institution to admit a transfer student to junior status in a baccalaureate degree program if the student completed a transfer degree or state minimum core curriculum.
Source
Ark. Code Ann. § 6-61-231
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
