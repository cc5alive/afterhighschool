73:Common Data Set 2025-2026
74:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
75:The | 99:Common Data Set (CDS) | 242:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in
74:the higher education community and publishers as represented by the | 449:College Board | 530:,
75:Peterson’s | 136:, and | 167:U.S. News & World Report | 316:. The combined goal of this collaboration is
74:to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s
74:transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data
75:providers.
75:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey
75:instrument or set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys
75:conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as
75:unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in
74:that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
75:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the
75:cohorts specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to
75:CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by
75:distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other
75:effective means.
74:Further information about the CDS initiative can be found at | 394:https://commondataset.org/ | 552:.
74:Please contact Peterson's Research at | 284:research@petersons.com | 425:should you have any
74:questions about this PDF template.
