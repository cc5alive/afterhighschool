Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Wyoming

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
In cooperation with the community colleges and the University of Wyoming, develop and maintain a common transcript and transfer process system that uses common course numbering for all undergraduate courses provided at the community colleges and the University of Wyoming.
Source
Wyo. Stat. Ann. § 21-18-202
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Wyoming does not have a transferable core of lower-division courses; however, any Wyoming institution of higher education that receives funding from the state of Wyoming has to accept any credit completed at another Wyoming institution of higher education, with a few exceptions up to the discretion of the receiving institution.
Source
Wyo. Stat. Ann. § 21-16-1701
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Students who have completed an associate of arts, associate of science, or associate of business degree from a Wyoming Community College receive credit toward completion of the lower division general education requirements included in the University Studies Program with exception of the First-Year Seminar requirement. Graduates must have completed a minimum of 64 college-level credits with a minimum of 2.0 GPA.
Source
Wyoming Community College Articulation Agreement
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
There is not a statute for reverse transfer; however, students who attend the University of Wyoming could be eligible for reverse transfer if they have completed at least 60 credits.
