55:Need-based aid: | 109:College-funded or college-administered award from institutional, state, federal, or other sources for which a student
55:must have financial need to qualify. This includes both institutional and non-institutional student aid (grants, jobs, and loans).
55:Need-based scholarship or grant aid: | 178:Scholarships and grants from institutional, state, federal, or other sources for which a student
55:must have financial need to qualify.
55:Need-based self-help aid: | 139:Loans and jobs from institutional, state, federal, or other sources for which a student must demonstrate
55:financial need to qualify.
55:Non-need-based scholarship or grant aid: | 193:Scholarships and grants, gifts, or merit-based aid from institutional, state, federal, or other
55:sources (including unrestricted funds or gifts and endowment income) awarded solely on the basis of academic achievement, merit, or
55:any other non-need-based reason. When reporting questions H1 and H2, non-need-based aid that is used to meet need should be
55:counted as need-based aid.
