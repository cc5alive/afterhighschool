42:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
42:The | 64:Common Data Set (CDS) | 195:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher
42:education community and publishers as represented by the | 333:College Board | 407:, | 413:Peterson’s | 470:, and | 497:U.S.
42:News & World Report | 154:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy
42:of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as well as to
42:reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
42:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of
42:data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating
42:publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher.
42:Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
42:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified
42:when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding
42:to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting
42:them on their websites, or by other effective means.
42:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns
42:AA–AL | 78:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 398:Answer Sheet | 471:. The Answer
42:Sheet includes field mappings to the | 221:U.S. News fillable PDF | 341:and to the | 393:Word | 424:version. Please confirm
42:with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of your responses. For more information
42:about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit | 286:https://commondataset.org/
42:Please contact the | 135:College Board College Survey | 295:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should
42:you have any questions about this Excel template.
42:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 318:Excel | 350:template can be matched to a | 498:U.S.
42:News fillable PDF | 136:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer
42:Sheet. Please confirm all questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another
42:organization.
42:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 272:Word | 303:template do not map directly—or require
42:different formatting—to an equivalent field in the | 278:U.S. News fillable PDF | 398:and in the | 449:Excel | 481:template.
42:An example are those questions with multiple category options such as "Very Important, Important,
42:Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these questions may appear as duplicates (light
42:red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code value
42:(c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses, and
42:mapping requirements before exporting to CSV or submitting to another organization.
