Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Hawaii

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, there is common course numbering at the community college level.
Source
UHCC Policy 5.301
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Courses numbered 100-499 will transfer as baccalaureate-level credit.
Source
UH System Policy E5.209
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Completing an associate of arts from a UHCC fulfills admission and lower division general education core requirements at all UH baccalaureate degree-granting institutions.
Source
UH System Policy E5.209
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The policy allows community college students who transferred to a UH four-year institution before completing their associate of arts or associate of science degree to obtain that credential while progressing toward their bachelor’s degree.
Source
UHCC Policy 5.206
