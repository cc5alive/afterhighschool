Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Tennessee

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The Tennessee Higher Education Commission in consultation with the governing boards of all state institutions of higher education shall develop a common course numbering system.
Source
Tenn. Code Ann. § 49-7-202(3A)
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
A transfer pathway exists that requires a 41 hour lower-division general education core common to all state colleges and universities that is transferable as a block to satisfy the general education core of any public community college or university, as well as 19 hours of pre-major or elective courses. Together, these 60 hours apply toward the requirements of a bachelor's degree at the public institution that offers the transfer pathway.
Source

Tenn. Code Ann. § 49-7-202 (2A)
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
A student who earned an associate of arts or associate of science degree from a Tennessee community college is deemed to have met all general education and university parallel core requirements for transfer to a Tennessee public university as a junior. Admission to certain programs, schools or colleges within the university remain competitive.
Source
Tenn. Code. Ann. § 49-7-202(2b)
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The community colleges of the Board of Regents System are authorized and encouraged to enter into reverse articulation or reverse transfer agreements with the universities of the Board of Regents and the University of Tennessee systems and with private institutions that are accredited by the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools. The universities of the Board of Regents and the University of Tennessee systems are authorized and encouraged to enter into reverse articulation or reverse transfer agreements with the community colleges of the Board of Regents system.
Source
Tenn. Code. Ann. § 49-7-150
