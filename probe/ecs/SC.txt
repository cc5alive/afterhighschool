Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - South Carolina

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the state has a course equivalency database.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Statewide Articulation agreement of 86 courses is applicable to all public institutions for transfer from two- to four-year public institutions.
Source
South Carolina Transfer and Articulation Center Policy
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Transfer blocks will be accepted in their totality toward meeting baccalaureate degree requirements at all four-year public institutions in relevant four-year degree programs. Any student who has completed an associate of arts or associate of science degree at any public two-year South Carolina institutions which contains the total coursework found in the Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences or the Science and Mathematics Transfer Block will automatically be entitled to junior-level status at whatever public senior institution they have been admitted to.
Source
South Carolina Commission on Higher Education Academic Transfer Articulation Policy
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Does not have reverse transfer policy.
