State Profile - Wisconsin

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, has a tool called the Transfer Wizard, which allows students to see which courses from their current institution will transfer and what they will transfer as.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Starting with the 2022-23 school year, the statute required the Board of Regents, the Technical College System Board and the governing boards of the tribally controlled colleges enter into and implement an agreement that identifies core general education courses totaling not fewer than 72 credits and establishes policies to ensure credits are transferable and would satisfy general education requirements at receiving institutions.
Source

Wis. Stat. Ann. § 36.31
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
A student who has earned an associate degree containing those system-wide requirements from an institution in the University of Wisconsin System and transfers to another institution in the system will be considered as having fulfilled the general education distribution or breadth requirements of the university.
Source
Regent Policy Document 7-1
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is intended for students who transfer from one associate degree granting UW institution to another UW System institution without completing the associate's degree. UW colleges may enter into cooperating agreements with other UW institutions, but not required. Students must have earned at least 36 credits at UW Colleges.
Source
UW System Administrative Policy 135
