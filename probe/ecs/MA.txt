Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Massachusetts

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Provides any student in the Massachusetts public higher education system the ability to complete a portable general education block to satisfy general education requirements at any public institution.
Source
Board Policy 11 AAC 16-20
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
A MOU between the presidents of Massachusetts Community Colleges, State Universities, and the University of Massachusetts guarantees transfer of credit for students enrolled in one of the approved 16 pathways, admission and a tuition discount to a linked baccalaureate program.
Source
MOU
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Credits earned at the four-year
institutions are transferred back to the community colleges and used to fulfill the
associate’s degree requirements. Once degree requirements are met, the community
colleges confer the associate’s degrees.
Source
Board Policy 11 AAC 17-08
