State Profile - Kansas

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
Each course in the general core is identified by a shared course number. The Kansas Regents Shared Number (KRSN) uses a 3-letter prefix and a 4-digit course number.
Source
Board Policy Ch. 3 Section 2
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Transfer of general education to and among Kansas public universities, including state universities and Washburn University, shall be accepted as having satisfied the general education requirements for the bachelor degree of all Kansas public universities. Students may not be required to take additional general education requirements. Credits can also transfer on a course-by-course basis.
Source
Board Policy Ch. 3 Section 2
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Students transferring to Kansas public universities with a completed AA, AFA, or AS degree shall be given junior standing.
Source
Board Policy Ch. 3 Section 2
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The institutions must work together with the System Council of Presidents and System Council of Chief Academic Officers to develop a reverse transfer process. Guidelines should be created to decide which transferring institution will award the credential if the student transferred from more than one institution.
Source
Board Policy Ch. 3 Section 3
