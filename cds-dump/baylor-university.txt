55:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
55:The | 77:Common Data Set (CDS) | 208:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher
55:education community and publishers as represented by the | 346:College Board | 420:, | 426:Peterson’s | 483:, and | 510:U.S.
55:News & World Report | 167:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and
55:accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as
55:well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
55:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set
55:of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the
55:participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to
55:each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of
55:items from the CDS.
55:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts
55:specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when
55:responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their
55:answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
55:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in
55:columns | 99:AA–AL | 135:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 455:Answer Sheet | 528:.
55:The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to the | 296:U.S. News fillable PDF | 415:and to the | 467:Word
55:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of your
55:responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
55:https://commondataset.org/
55:Please contact the | 148:College Board College Survey | 308:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org
55:should you have any questions about this Excel template.
55:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 331:Excel | 363:template can be matched to a | 511:U.S.
55:News fillable PDF | 149:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the
55:Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to
55:another organization.
55:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 285:Word | 316:template do not map directly—or require
55:different formatting—to an equivalent field in the | 291:U.S. News fillable PDF | 411:and in the | 462:Excel
55:template. An example are those questions with multiple category options such as "Very Important,
55:Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these questions may appear as
55:duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code value
55:(c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses, and
55:mapping requirements before exporting to CSV or submitting to another organization.
