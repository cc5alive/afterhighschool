Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Iowa

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, there is common course numbering at the community college level.
Source
IAC 281.21.4
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Does not have transferable core lower-division courses.
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Requires the directors of each community college to enter into a collective statewide articulation agreement with the state board of regents to provide for seamless transfer of academic credits from a completed associate of arts or associate of science offered by a community college to a baccalaureate degree program offered by any institution governed by the state board of regents.
Source
Iowa Code § 260C.14-22
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The community colleges and public four-year institutions have an agreement providing pathways for reverse transfer. The agreement is a partnership between the Board of Regents and Iowa's public universities and collaborating with community colleges. Students can obtain a degree, diploma or certification.
Source
Reverse Credit Transfer Agreement
