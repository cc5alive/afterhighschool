Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Alabama

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the state has a course equivalency database.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Articulation and General Studies Committee must create a statewide freshman and sophomore level general studies curriculum to be accepted at all public colleges and universities. However, institutions may require additional general studies courses beyond the statewide general studies curriculum.
Source
Ala. Code § 16-5-8
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree.
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Has a memorandum of understanding by and between the Alabama Community College System colleges and universities within the state of Alabama who elect to participate in the exchange of information providing seamless transfer process for students meeting criteria for the completion of their approved associate degree.
Source
Memorandum of Understanding
