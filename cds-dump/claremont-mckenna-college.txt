77:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
77:The | 94:Common Data Set (CDS) | 194:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education community
77:and publishers as represented by the | 217:College Board | 274:, | 278:Peterson’s | 321:, and | 342:U.S. News & World Report | 446:. The combined goal
77:of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition
77:into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
77:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented in
77:a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the
77:CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize
77:varying numbers of items from the CDS.
77:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when answering
77:CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests
77:they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
77:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 458:AA–AL | 486:. Before
77:exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 288:Answer Sheet | 344:. The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to
77:the | 91:U.S. News fillable PDF | 181:and to the | 221:Word | 245:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to
77:accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
77:https://commondataset.org/
77:Please contact the | 148:College Board College Survey | 270:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any
77:questions about this Excel template.

77:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions,
77:responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another organization.
77:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 252:Word | 276:template do not map directly—or require different formatting—to an
77:equivalent field in the | 158:U.S. News fillable PDF | 248:and in the | 287:Excel | 312:template. An example are those questions with multiple
77:category options such as "Very Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these
