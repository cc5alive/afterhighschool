State Profile - Alaska

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
A student who completed the general education requirements at one university system university or community college and transfers to another system university or community college will be considered to have completed the general education requirements at all University of Alaska universities and community colleges.
Source
Board Policy 10.04.062
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however, the policy says institutions are "encouraged" to develop degree programs that build upon a student's prior experience, and may include associate degrees.
Source
Board Policy 10.04.064
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Does not have statewide reverse transfer policy.
