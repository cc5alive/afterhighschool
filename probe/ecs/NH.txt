Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - New Hampshire

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however the state has a course equivalency database.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Does not have transferable core lower-division courses; however, NHTransfer serves as a resource to connect students to the options available to transfer between community college and University System of New Hampshire institution.

Additionally, beginning in July 2024, the university system of New Hampshire will be changing their policy regarding lower division requirements.
Source
N.H. Rev. Stat. § 187-A:16-c
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however there are articulation agreements among certain institutions in the state that allow students with 60 credits to enter a bachelor's program with junior status.

Additionally, beginning in July 2024, the university of New Hampshire system will be changing their policy regarding transferring associate degrees.
Source
N.H. Rev. Stat. § 187-A:16-c
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Does not have reverse transfer policy.
