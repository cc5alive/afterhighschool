46:Common Data Set 2025-2026
46:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
46:The | 68:Common Data Set (CDS) | 200:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher
46:education community and publishers as represented by the | 337:College Board | 412:, | 418:Peterson’s | 474:, and | 502:U.S.
46:News & World Report | 159:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy
46:of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as well as to
46:reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
46:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of
46:data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating
46:publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher.
46:Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of items from the
46:CDS.
46:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified
46:when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding
46:to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting
46:them on their websites, or by other effective means.
46:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in
46:columns | 90:AA–AL | 126:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 447:Answer Sheet | 520:. The
46:Answer Sheet includes field mappings to the | 266:U.S. News fillable PDF | 385:and to the | 437:Word | 468:version. Please
46:confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of your responses. For more
46:information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit | 348:https://commondataset.org/
46:Please contact the | 140:College Board College Survey | 300:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should
46:you have any questions about this Excel template.
46:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 323:Excel | 355:template can be matched to a | 503:U.S.
46:News fillable PDF | 141:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer
46:Sheet. Please confirm all questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another
46:organization.
46:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 277:Word | 308:template do not map directly—or require
46:different formatting—to an equivalent field in the | 283:U.S. News fillable PDF | 402:and in the | 454:Excel | 485:template.
46:An example are those questions with multiple category options such as "Very Important, Important,
46:Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these questions may appear as duplicates (light
46:red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code value
