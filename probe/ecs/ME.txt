State Profile - Maine

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the University of Maine system has a uniform course numbering system.
Source
Me. Rev. Stat. tit. 20-A, § 10907
Board Policy 304.1
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Does not have transferable core lower-division courses.
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
A person who earns an associate degree from the Maine Community College System must be allowed to transfer credits earned to the University of Maine System for use toward a baccalaureate degree.
Source
Me. Rev. Stat. tit. 20-A, § 10907-A
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
A student who earns credits at the University of Maine System, but who does not earn a degree, must be allowed to transfer those credits to the Maine Community College System for use toward an associate degree.
Source
Me. Rev. Stat. tit. 20-A, § 10907-A
