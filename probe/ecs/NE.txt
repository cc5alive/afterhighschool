Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Nebraska

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the state has a course equivalency database.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
However, Transfer Nebraska provides a resource for students to determine transferability of the course credits they have earned.
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have a statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however, The Nebraska Transfer Initiative allows for the transfer of an associate degree. This is voluntary and not in policy. The Nebraska State College system specifies that the completion of at least 30 credit hours of an associate of arts or an associate of science will fulfill the general studies requirements at a Nebraska State College.
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
