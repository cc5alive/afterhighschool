Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Rhode Island

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the state has a course equivalency database.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Does not have transferable core lower-division courses.

However, when community college courses are developed, the community college must ensure that courses meet the articulation agreement and transfer to Rhode Island College or the University of Rhode Island.
Source
RIPOC policy S6
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The JAA allows students who start at CCRI to enroll in the agreement and be conditionally admitted to RIC or URI. Within this program a student must earn a 2.4 GPA and 60 credits that will be applied to a bachelor's degree.
Source
Joint Admissions Agreement (JAA)
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Students who attend the Community College of Rhode Island, Rhode Island College or the University of Rhode Island may qualify for the conferral of the associate degree or certificate from the Community College of Rhode Island through the reverse transfer policy if they meet eligibility requirements.
Source
RIPOC policy S15
