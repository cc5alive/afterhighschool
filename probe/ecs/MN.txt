State Profile - Minnesota

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The regents of the University of Minnesota and the trustees of the Minnesota State Colleges and Universities must develop and maintain a common numbering convention to distinguish remedial, lower division, upper division and graduate level coursework.
Source
Minn. Stat. Ann. § 135A.08
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Does not have transferable core lower-division courses, however Minnesota Transfer provides a resource to help students with the transfer process.
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however, has a policy that applies only to the Minnesota State system and does not apply to all associate degrees.
Source
Board Policy 3.21, Part 6, Subpart D
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The Minnesota State University system does have reverse transfer in place however, their program does not include the U of M system. The U of M system has a memorandum of agreement with community colleges.
