Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Maryland

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Maryland Higher Education Commission must develop and implement a statewide transfer agreement requiring at least 60 credits of general education, elective and major courses a student earns at a community college are transferable to any public institution for credit toward a bachelors degree.
Source
Md. Code Ann. § 11-207
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The Commission must develop and implement a statewide transfer agreement requiring at least 60 credits of general education, elective and major courses a student earns at a community college are transferable to any public institution for credit toward a bachelors degree.
Source
Md. Code, Com. Law § 11-207
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The Commission must develop and implement a statewide reverse transfer agreement where at least 30 credits a student earns at a public institution toward a bachelors degree are transferable to any community college toward an associates degree.
Source
Md. Stat. Tit. 11 § 207
