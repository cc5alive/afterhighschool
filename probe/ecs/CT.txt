Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Connecticut

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, there is common course numbering at the community college level.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Does not have a statewide transferable core of lower division courses. The Regional Community-Technical College System and the Connecticut State University System must develop and implement a general education core of courses for no less than thirty academic credits to be offered as part of its liberal arts and sciences programs and any other degree program designated as a transfer program. Policy does not include the University of Connecticut.
Source
Conn. Gen. Stat. Ann. § 10a-157
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have a statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree, however there is guaranteed transfer of an associate's degree between all community colleges and the Connecticut State Colleges & Universities. This does not include the UCONN system.
Source
CSCU Board of Regents Transfer and Articulation Policy 1.5
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Does not have a statewide reverse transfer policy. The Connecticut State University System must identify students eligible for reverse transfer, and share the information with community colleges. The policy does not include the University of Connecticut.
Source
CSCU Board of Regents Reverse Transfer Policy 1.14
