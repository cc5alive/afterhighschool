Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - New Mexico

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
By August 1, 2017, the Higher Education Department must establish a common course naming and numbering system of courses identified as substantially equivalent lower-division courses.
Source
N.M. Stat. Ann. § 21-1B-3
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Higher Education Department is authorized to define, publish and maintain a common core of lower-division general education. Aimed at facilitating the transfer of students among the state's post-secondary institutions. Consists of 35 semester hours of credit. Institutions can require additional credits for general education.
Source
N.M. Stat. Ann. § 21-1B-3
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The department shall develop and approve statewide meta-majors and transfer modules, as well as a statewide general education core curriculum. All courses within a transfer module are guaranteed to transfer to any New Mexico university and apply toward bachelor's degree program requirements.
Source
N.M. Stat. Ann. § 21-1B-3
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
