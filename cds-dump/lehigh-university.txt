55:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
55:The | 77:Common Data Set (CDS) | 209:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education
55:community and publishers as represented by the | 296:College Board | 370:, | 376:Peterson’s | 432:, and | 460:U.S. News & World Report | 597:. The
55:combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a
55:student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
55:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented
55:in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from
55:the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they
55:utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
55:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when answering
55:CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests
55:they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
55:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 557:AA–AL | 593:. Before
55:exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 334:Answer Sheet | 406:. The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to
55:the | 73:U.S. News fillable PDF | 193:and to the | 245:Word | 276:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to
55:accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
55:https://commondataset.org/
55:Please contact the | 149:College Board College Survey | 309:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any
55:questions about this Excel template.
