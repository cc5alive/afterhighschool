State Profile - Oregon

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The Higher Education Coordination commission will require each public postsecondary education institution to adopt a common course numbering system for introductory and other lower division courses.
Source
OAR 350.429
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Requires establishment of a foundational curriculum for the first year of coursework at public post-secondary institutions. Ensures that if a student at community college completed 90 academic credits, the student will receive junior status upon transferring to the public university. For students participating in a transfer program under the unified statewide transfer agreement, the agreement will dictate the number of credits that will transfer in order for the student to receive junior status and be able to receive their bachelor's degree by completing the additional credits outlined.
Source
OAR 350.400
OAR 350.404
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Defines an Associate of Arts Oregon Transfer (AAOT) degree as a state-approved associate degree that is intended to prepare students to transfer into upper division courses for a baccalaureate degree. Any student who earns an AAOT will have met the lower division general education requirements of any public university in Oregon. Students transfer with junior level status for registration purposes. Programs and majors may have additional requirements students need to meet.
Source
Higher Education Coordinating Commission 589-006-0050(6)
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The Transfer Student Bill of Rights and Responsibilities includes a process by which a community college can award an associate degree to a student upon completion of the required credits, regardless of whether the student applied to receive the degree or whether the student earned the credits for the degree at a community college or a state institution of higher education.
Source
Or. Rev. Stat. § 350.395
