30:Common Data Set 2025-2026
30:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
30:The | 52:Common Data Set (CDS) | 183:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education
30:community and publishers as represented by the | 270:College Board | 345:, | 351:Peterson’s | 407:, and | 435:U.S. News & World
30:Report | 66:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided
30:to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data
30:providers.
30:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data
30:represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers
30:incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the
30:publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
30:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when
30:answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the
30:numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their
30:websites, or by other effective means.
30:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 532:AA–
30:AL | 44:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 364:Answer Sheet | 437:. The Answer Sheet
30:includes field mappings to the | 178:U.S. News fillable PDF | 297:and to the | 349:Word | 380:version. Please confirm with the
30:receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the
30:Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit | 225:https://commondataset.org/
