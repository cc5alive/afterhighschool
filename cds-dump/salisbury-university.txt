67:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
67:The | 82:Common Data Set (CDS) | 176:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education community and
67:publishers as represented by the | 182:College Board | 235:, | 240:Peterson’s | 280:, and | 299:U.S. News & World Report | 396:. The combined goal of this
67:collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher
67:education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
67:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented in a
67:database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as
67:well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers
67:of items from the CDS.
67:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when answering CDS
67:items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by
67:distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
67:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 423:AA–AL | 449:. Before exporting,
67:verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 228:Answer Sheet | 280:. The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to the | 455:U.S. News
67:fillable PDF | 112:and to the | 148:Word | 171:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of your
67:responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit | 355:https://commondataset.org/
67:Please contact the | 133:College Board College Survey | 247:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any questions
67:about this Excel template.
67:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 263:Excel | 286:template can be matched to a | 391:U.S. News fillable PDF | 475:equivalent.
67:The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions, responses, and
67:mapping before submitting a CSV to another organization.
67:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 230:Word | 253:template do not map directly—or require different formatting—to an
67:equivalent field in the | 142:U.S. News fillable PDF | 227:and in the | 263:Excel | 286:template. An example are those questions with multiple category
67:options such as "Very Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these questions may appear as
67:duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code value (c7_state_residency_very_important ).
67:Please review and verify all questions, responses, and mapping requirements before exporting to CSV or submitting to another
67:organization.
