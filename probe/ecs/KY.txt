Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Kentucky

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, there is common course numbering at the community and technical college level.
Source
Ky. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 164.2951
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Community colleges can offer any and all lower division academic courses offered for undergraduate credit at any four year public college or university. All lower division courses offered at community colleges must be transferable for academic credit to all four year public colleges and universities.
Source
K.R.S. 164.583
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The Council on Postsecondary Education must facilitate the development and implementation of a statewide agreement for alignment of Kentucky Community and Technical College System lower-division associate of arts and associate of science coursework that must be accepted and fully credited to related bachelors degree programs. Guarantees that graduates of an associate of arts or associate of science program is deemed to have met all general education requirements. Students transferring are not required to complete any additional courses if completed an associate of arts or associate of science degree. These students also receive priority for admission over out-of-state students.
Source
Ky. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 164.2951
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
