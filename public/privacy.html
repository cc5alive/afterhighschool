afterhighschool.me

Privacy

Most people using this site are in high school, so we collect as little as we can and say exactly what happens to it.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist: your email address, whether you're a student, parent, counselor or something else, and the date you signed up. That's all.

What we use it for

To email you when the site's tools open, and occasionally with updates about the project. Every email has a way to unsubscribe.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't share it with colleges, advertisers or data brokers. We don't use advertising trackers.

Kids under 13

The waitlist is for people 13 and older. If you think a child under 13 has signed up, email us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your information

Email hello@afterhighschool.me from the address you signed up with and we'll remove it.

Analytics

We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.
