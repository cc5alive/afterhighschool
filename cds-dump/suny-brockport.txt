89:Welcome to the 2023-2024 Common Data Set collection!
89:The | 111:Common Data Set (CDS) | 242:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in
89:the higher education community and publishers as represented by the | 424:College Board | 497:,
89:Peterson’s | 142:, and | 168:U.S. News & World Report | 307:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to
89:improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s
89:transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
89:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument
89:or set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by
89:the participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items
89:proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they
89:utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
89:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the
89:cohorts specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to
89:CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing
89:photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
85:Further information about the CDS initiative can be found at | 377:https://commondataset.org/ | 515:.
