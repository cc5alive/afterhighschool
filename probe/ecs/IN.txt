Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Indiana

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The commission shall develop, implement, and maintain a common course numbering system to be used by the state educational institutions for all courses in the core transfer library. This common course numbering system must be mapped to the unique course numbers used at each institution.
Source
Ind. Code Ann. § 21-18-9-7
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Each institution must create a statewide transfer general education core based on a set of core competencies comprising at least 30 credit hours that apply toward undergraduate degrees. Credits earned within the core must be transferable among all state educational institutions. A student who has satisfactorily completed the statewide transfer general education core at a state institution may not be required to complete additional course in the general education core at the receiving institution. A student who holds an associate degree approved by the commission is considered to have met at least 30 credit hours of the general education requirement at the receiving institution.
Source
Ind. Code. § 21-42-3-1 et seq
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Requires each educational institution to create a single articulation pathway for programmatic areas in which significant numbers of students may first obtain an associate of science or associate of arts with the intent of obtaining a bachelor degree. The institutions must apply all the credits earned for the student's associate degree toward the related baccalaureate degree so that the student may begin the baccalaureate degree as a junior status student as long as it has been identified as a single articulation pathway program.
Source
Ind. Code § 21-42-6-4
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
