92:The | 116:Common Data Set (CDS) | 259:initiative is a collaborative effort among data
92:providers in the higher education community and publishers as represented by the
92:College Board | 174:, | 180:Peterson’s | 242:, and | 271:U.S. News & World Report | 419:. The combined goal
92:of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided
92:to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce
92:the reporting burden on data providers.
92:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey
92:instrument or set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education
92:surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS
92:as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’
92:surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
92:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and
92:the cohorts specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the
92:answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests they
92:receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their
92:websites, or by other effective means.
