Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Mississippi

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, there is common course numbering at the community college level.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Students who graduate with an associate degree from a Mississippi community or junior college and complete the 30-hour IHL Board core curriculum with a "C" or higher will satisfy the IHL core at the institution. However, general education requirements for BA programs at receiving institutions may exceed the minimum IHL core curriculum.
Source
Board Policy Section 517
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
IHL institutions will accept all transferable courses with a grade of "C" or higher completed as part of an associate degree. Once transferred, students can begin taking courses within a declared major or to fulfill prerequisites for a major.
Source
Board Policy Section 517
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Have an agreement between the community colleges and all public universities to process reverse transfers. Students must earn at least 16 credits from a single MCCB college, earn at least 62 credits combined from MCCB and an IHL university, be in good financial standing at the IHL, and agree to allow the IHL university to automatically provide his/her transcript to a MCCB college.
Source
Mississippi Board of Trustees Policy
