Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Pennsylvania

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the state has a course equivalency database for PA TRAC participating schools.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses

The Transfer and Articulation Oversight Committee (TAOC) created the 30-Credit Transfer Framework which allows students to transfer up to 30 credits of foundational courses from 2 year programs to 4-year programs.
Source
Policy 1999-01-A
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Public institutions of higher learning must accept students who complete an associate of arts or an associate of science degree into the parallel baccalaureate program.
Source
24 P.S. § 20-2002-C
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
There is a statewide reverse transfer agreement in place for students who did not complete their associate degrees while at the community college and transferred to a Pennsylvania State System University school. Credits earned while pursuing a Bachelor's degree will be sent back to the Pennsylvania community college for evaluation of completion of the associate degree.
Source

Policy 1999-01-A
