76:The | 98:Common Data Set (CDS) | 229:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education
76:community and publishers as represented by the | 316:College Board | 391:, | 397:Peterson’s | 453:, and | 481:U.S. News & World Report | 618:. The
76:combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a
76:student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
76:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented
76:in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from
76:the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they
76:utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
76:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when answering
76:CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests
76:they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
