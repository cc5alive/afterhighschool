Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Michigan

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
However, the state funded the MiTransfer Pathways project creating guaranteed transfer pathways between two- and four-year institutions.
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however, the Michigan Transfer Agreement allows for the transfer of a minimum of 30 credits at participating institutions. Board policy specifies that a receiving college or university is only required to accept courses defined within formal transfer agreements and Transfer Pathways that it has with a sending college or university.
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Community colleges are encouraged work with public universities to implement statewide reverse transfer agreements to increase the number of students awarded credentials of value upon completion of required credits. The agreements must enable students who have earned a significant number of credits at a community college and transferred to a four-year institution before completing a degree to transfer the credits earned at the four-year back to the community college to be awarded a credential of value.
Source
M.C.A.A. § 388.1810d
