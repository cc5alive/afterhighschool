68:Common Data Set 2025-2026
68:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
68:The | 90:Common Data Set (CDS) | 222:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the
68:higher education community and publishers as represented by the | 393:College Board | 468:, | 474:Peterson’s | 530:,
68:and | 90:U.S. News & World Report | 227:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the
68:quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher
68:education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
68:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or
68:set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the
68:participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to
68:each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers
68:of items from the CDS.
68:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts
68:specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items
68:when responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of
68:their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
68:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in
68:columns | 112:AA–AL | 148:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 469:Answer
68:Sheet | 98:. The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to the | 346:U.S. News fillable PDF | 465:and to the
68:Word | 99:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of
68:your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
68:https://commondataset.org/
68:Please contact the | 162:College Board College Survey | 322:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org
68:should you have any questions about this Excel template.
68:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 345:Excel | 377:template can be matched to a
68:U.S. News fillable PDF | 187:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on
68:the Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a
68:CSV to another organization.
68:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 299:Word | 330:template do not map directly—or require
68:different formatting—to an equivalent field in the | 305:U.S. News fillable PDF | 424:and in the | 476:Excel
68:template. An example are those questions with multiple category options such as "Very
68:Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these questions may
68:appear as duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code
68:value (c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses,
68:and mapping requirements before exporting to CSV or submitting to another organization.
