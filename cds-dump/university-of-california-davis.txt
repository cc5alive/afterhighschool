89:Common Data Set 2025-2026
89:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
89:The | 106:Common Data Set (CDS) | 206:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education community
89:and publishers as represented by the | 229:College Board | 286:, | 290:Peterson’s | 333:, and | 354:U.S. News & World Report | 458:. The combined goal
89:of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition
89:into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
89:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented in
89:a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the
89:CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize
89:varying numbers of items from the CDS.
89:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when answering
89:CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests
89:they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
89:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 471:AA–AL | 498:. Before
89:exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 300:Answer Sheet | 356:. The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to
89:the | 103:U.S. News fillable PDF | 193:and to the | 233:Word | 257:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to
89:accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
89:https://commondataset.org/
89:Please contact the | 160:College Board College Survey | 282:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any
89:questions about this Excel template.
89:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 299:Excel | 323:template can be matched to a | 436:U.S. News fillable PDF
89:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions,
89:responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another organization.
89:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 264:Word | 288:template do not map directly—or require different formatting—to an
89:equivalent field in the | 170:U.S. News fillable PDF | 260:and in the | 300:Excel | 324:template. An example are those questions with multiple
89:category options such as "Very Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these
89:questions may appear as duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code value
89:(c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses, and mapping requirements
89:before exporting to CSV or submitting to another organization.
