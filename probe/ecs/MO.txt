Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Missouri

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The coordinating board for higher education shall establish a standard core curriculum and a common course numbering equivalency matrix for lower-division courses to be used at community colleges and other public institutions of higher education to facilitate student transfers.
Source
Mo. Ann. Stat. § 178.780 (10)
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Requires all public two-year and four-year institutions to create a statewide core transfer library of at least twenty-five lower division courses that are transferable among all public institutions. The higher education core curriculum transfer act expands this to a 42 credit hour block transferable to any public institution in the state.
Source
Mo. Rev. Stat. § 173.005 (9)
Mo. Ann. Stat. § 178.785
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The associate of arts degree is designed as a statewide general studies transfer degree. Students who complete the associate of arts degree will have completed a general education program that is consistent with the statewide general education policy which has a 42 semester hour block of courses. Depending on the courses taken and the bachelors degree they transfer to they could need to complete additional courses required for that major.
Source
Mo. Ann. Stat. § 178.785 to 178.789
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The coordinating board must develop a policy to foster reverse transfer for any student who has accumulated enough hours in combination with at least one public institution in Missouri that offers an associate degree and one public four-year institution in the prescribed courses to meet the institution's requirements to be awarded the associate degree.
Source
Mo. Rev. Stat. § 173.005 (9)
