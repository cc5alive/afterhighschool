56:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
56:The | 78:Common Data Set (CDS) | 209:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the
56:higher education community and publishers as represented by the | 380:College Board | 455:, | 461:Peterson’s | 518:,
56:and | 77:U.S. News & World Report | 214:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the
56:quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher
56:education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
56:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set
56:of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the
56:participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to
56:each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers
56:of items from the CDS.
56:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts
56:specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items
56:when responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of
56:their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other effective means.
56:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in
56:columns | 100:AA–AL | 136:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 456:Answer
56:Sheet | 86:. The Answer Sheet includes field mappings to the | 333:U.S. News fillable PDF | 453:and to the
56:Word | 87:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of
56:your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
56:https://commondataset.org/
56:Please contact the | 149:College Board College Survey | 309:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org
56:should you have any questions about this Excel template.
56:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 332:Excel | 364:template can be matched to a
56:U.S. News fillable PDF | 175:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the
56:Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to
56:another organization.
56:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 286:Word | 317:template do not map directly—or require
56:different formatting—to an equivalent field in the | 292:U.S. News fillable PDF | 412:and in the | 463:Excel
56:template. An example are those questions with multiple category options such as "Very
56:Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for these questions may

56:value (c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses,
