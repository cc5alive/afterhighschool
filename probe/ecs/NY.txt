State Profile - New York

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering.

SUNY has a course equivalencies table for transferring within the system.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
No
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
There are two different system policies for State University New York and City University New York.

CUNY: Common core program is a 30 credit general education requirement that transfers to any CUNY school.

SUNY: SUNY has a General Education Requirement (SUNY-GER) that is transferable between institutions.
Source
CUNY policy 1.28
SUNY Trustees Resolution 2010-006
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
No
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Does not have statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree; however, both the SUNY and CUNY systems have transfer policies.

CUNY: In order to complete the Associate's Degree, CUNY students are required to complete the common core general education requirement. The common core is guaranteed to transfer.

SUNY: Graduates with an associates from the SUNY system shall be guaranteed junior status. New York residents from City University two year programs are guaranteed the same opportunities to continue at SUNY school as SUNY graduates.
Source

CUNY policy 1.28
SUNY Trustees Resolution 2009-138
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
The state university and city university systems each provide reverse transfer within their systems.

CUNY: reverse transfer exists between CUNY institutions.

SUNY: SUNY students can transfer their credits back to a two-year institution to see if they qualify for an Associate's degree.
