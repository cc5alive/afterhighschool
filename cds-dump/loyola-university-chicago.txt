66:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
66:The | 88:Common Data Set (CDS) | 220:initiative is a collaborative effort among data
66:providers in the higher education community and publishers as represented
66:by the | 99:College Board | 174:, | 180:Peterson’s | 236:, and | 264:U.S. News & World Report | 401:. The
66:combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of
66:information provided to all involved in a student’s transition into higher
66:education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
66:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a
66:survey instrument or set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher
66:education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates
66:items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher.
66:Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying
66:numbers of items from the CDS.
66:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions
66:and the cohorts specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to
66:use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey
66:requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting
66:them on their websites, or by other effective means.
66:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab,
66:responses are stored in columns | 228:AA–AL | 264:. Before exporting, verify that your
66:entries appear correctly on the | 218:Answer Sheet | 291:. The Answer Sheet includes
66:field mappings to the | 171:U.S. News fillable PDF | 290:and to the | 342:Word | 373:version.
66:Please confirm with the receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV
66:of your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS)
66:initiative, visit | 134:https://commondataset.org/
66:Please contact the | 160:College Board College Survey | 320:team at
66:collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any questions about this
66:Excel template.

66:questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another
66:organization.
