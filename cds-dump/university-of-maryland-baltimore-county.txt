110:The | 132:Common Data Set (CDS) | 263:initiative is a collaborative effort among data
110:providers in the higher education community and publishers as represented by the
110:College Board | 185:, | 191:Peterson’s | 247:, and | 274:U.S. News & World Report | 409:. The combined goal
110:of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided
110:to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce
110:the reporting burden on data providers.
110:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey
110:instrument or set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education
110:surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS
110:as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’
110:surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
110:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and
110:the cohorts specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the
110:answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests they
110:receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their
110:websites, or by other effective means.
