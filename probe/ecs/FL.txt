Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Florida

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The Department of Education, in conjunction with the Board of Governors, shall develop, coordinate, and maintain a statewide course numbering system for postsecondary and dual enrollment education in school districts, public postsecondary educational institutions, and participating nonpublic postsecondary educational institutions.
Source
Fla. Stat. Ann. § 1007.24
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The chair of the State Board of Education and of the Board of Governors must appoint faculty committees to identify statewide general education core course options. All public postsecondary institutions must accept these courses as meeting general education core course requirements.
Source
FL. Stat. § 1007.25
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The State Board of Education and the Board of Governors must enter into a statewide articulation agreement. The articulation agreement must specifically provide that every associate in arts graduate will have met all general education requirements and must be granted admission to the upper division of a state university or Florida College System institution.
Source
Fla. Stat. § 1007.23
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Students at state universities may request associate in arts certificates if they have successfully completed the minimum requirements for the degree. The university must grant the student an associate in arts degree if the student has successfully completed minimum requirements for the associate in arts degree.
Source
Fla. Stat. § 1007.25
