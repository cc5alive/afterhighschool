113:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
113:The | 135:Common Data Set (CDS) | 266:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education
113:community and publishers as represented by the | 353:College Board | 428:, | 434:Peterson’s | 490:, and | 518:U.S. News & World Report | 655:. The
113:combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a
113:student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
113:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented
113:in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items
113:from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in
113:that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
113:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when
113:answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous
113:survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other
113:effective means.
113:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 615:AA–AL | 651:.
113:Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 427:Answer Sheet | 499:. The Answer Sheet includes field
113:mappings to the | 194:U.S. News fillable PDF | 314:and to the | 366:Word | 397:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that
113:it is able to accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
113:https://commondataset.org/
113:Please contact the | 207:College Board College Survey | 367:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any
113:questions about this Excel template.
113:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 390:Excel | 421:template can be matched to a | 569:U.S. News fillable
113:PDF | 138:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer Sheet. Please confirm all
113:questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another organization.
113:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 344:Word | 375:template do not map directly—or require different formatting—
113:to an equivalent field in the | 247:U.S. News fillable PDF | 366:and in the | 418:Excel | 449:template. An example are those questions with
113:multiple category options such as "Very Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for
113:these questions may appear as duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code
113:value (c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses, and mapping
113:requirements before exporting to CSV or submitting to another organization.
