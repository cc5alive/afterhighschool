Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Louisiana

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
Yes
Description of statewide common course numbering
The Board of Regents must develop, coordinate and maintain a statewide course numbering system for postsecondary and dual enrollment education at all public institutions.
Source
La. Stat. Ann. § 17:3164
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Board of Regents must identify courses that meet general education requirements in each major area. All public institutions must accept the general education courses.
Source
LSA-R.S. 17:3165
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
The Board of Regents must create statewide articulation agreements that guarantee that students who earned an associate of arts or associate of science and transfer to a four-year institution are deemed to have met all general education and other core curriculum requirements and must be admitted to the upper division of the four-year. If the associate of arts or associate of science degree is approved by the council for transfer the student is not required to take any additional general education courses. Students also receive priority admission over out-of-state students.
Source
La. Stat. Ann. § 17:3163
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Four-year colleges and universities and community colleges shall enter into reverse articulation or reverse transfer agreements to facilitate the transfer of academic credits earned by a student while enrolled in the four-year back to a community college to award an associate degree.
Source
La. Stat. Ann. § 17:3161.1
