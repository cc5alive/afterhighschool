Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - New Jersey

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, the state has a course equivalency database.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Each public institution of higher education must enter into a collective statewide transfer agreement to priced the seamless transfer of academic credits to a baccalaureate degree program. Transfer agreements include a listing of the general education of core courses as stipulated by the Presidents' council.
Source
N.J. Rev. Stat. § 18A:62-46
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Each public institution must establish and enter into a collective statewide agreement providing seamless transfer of academic credits from a completed associate of arts or associate of science degree to a baccalaureate degree program. The agreement must include a list of general education core courses, policies and procedures for transfer and application of academic credits from a completed associate of arts or associate of science degree program to a baccalaureate program, policies and procedures for the implementation of an appeals process, policies and procedures for an annual review and update of the agreement and policies and procedures for data collection.
Source
N.J. Stat. Ann. § 18A:62-46
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Each institution shall establish and enter into a collective statewide reverse transfer agreement. A student enrolled in a four-year institutions who accumulated a total of 66 credits between a county college and a four-year institution may be awarded an associate degree by the county college.
Source
NJ Rev Stat § 18A:62-46.1
