Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - West Virginia

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering.
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
The Commission and the Council must maintain a core coursework transfer agreement. This agreement lists the general studies courses at each institution which have been approved for inclusion in the agreement and is updated annually. A student may transfer up to thirty five credit hours of undergraduate coursework in the areas of English composition, communications and literature, fine arts appreciation, mathematics, natural science, and social science as general studies credits.
Source
Procedural Rule 133-17-3.2.k
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
With the exception of those enrolling in specialized four-year programs, students completing two-year associate degrees at public institutions in the state are generally transferred in at the junior level status and be able to graduate with the same number of total credit hours as a non-transfer student.
Source
Procedural Rule 133-17-3.2g
Statewide reverse transfer
Yes
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Establishes the process by which a student may be awarded an associate degree which was begun at a West Virginia public institution offering an associate degree but was completed through a West Virginia public four-year college or university. Student must have successfully completed a minimum of 15 credit hours at the two-year institution. All WV public higher education institutions must participate.
Source
Procedural Rule 133-60
