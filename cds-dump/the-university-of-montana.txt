74:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
74:The | 98:Common Data Set (CDS) | 242:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in
74:the higher education community and publishers as represented by the | 449:College Board | 530:,
74:Peterson’s | 136:, and | 165:U.S. News & World Report | 314:. The combined goal of this collaboration is
74:to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a student’s
74:transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data
74:providers.
74:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey
74:instrument or set of data represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys
74:conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from the CDS as well as
74:unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in
74:that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
74:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the
74:cohorts specified when answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to
74:CDS items when responding to the numerous survey requests they receive, by
74:distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other
74:effective means.
73:Further information about the CDS initiative can be found at | 392:https://commondataset.org/ | 547:.
73:Please contact Peterson's Research at research@petersons.com should you have any
73:questions about this PDF template.
