89:Common Data Set 2025-2026
89:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
89:The | 106:Common Data Set (CDS) | 209:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education
89:community and publishers as represented by the | 276:College Board | 335:, | 339:Peterson’s | 383:, and | 405:U.S. News & World Report | 512:. The
89:combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided to all involved in a
89:student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data providers.
89:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data represented
89:in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers incorporates items from
89:the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the publishers’ surveys differ in that
89:they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
89:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when
89:answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the numerous
89:survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their websites, or by other
89:effective means.
89:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 481:AA–AL | 509:.
89:Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 334:Answer Sheet | 390:. The Answer Sheet includes field
89:mappings to the | 153:U.S. News fillable PDF | 245:and to the | 286:Word | 310:version. Please confirm with the receiving organization that
89:it is able to accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit
89:https://commondataset.org/
89:Please contact the | 162:College Board College Survey | 287:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have any
89:questions about this Excel template.
89:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 305:Excel | 330:template can be matched to a | 445:U.S. News fillable PDF
89:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer Sheet. Please confirm all questions,
89:responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another organization.
89:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 269:Word | 293:template do not map directly—or require different formatting—to
89:an equivalent field in the | 184:U.S. News fillable PDF | 277:and in the | 317:Excel | 342:template. An example are those questions with
89:multiple category options such as "Very Important, Important, Considered, Not Considered". The matching cells for
89:these questions may appear as duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet) or a single category is listed as the code
89:value (c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify all questions, responses, and mapping
89:requirements before exporting to CSV or submitting to another organization.
