57:Common Data Set 2025-2026
57:Welcome to the 2025-2026 Common Data Set collection!
57:The | 77:Common Data Set (CDS) | 194:initiative is a collaborative effort among data providers in the higher education
57:community and publishers as represented by the | 271:College Board | 338:, | 343:Peterson’s | 393:, and | 418:U.S. News & World
57:Report | 89:. The combined goal of this collaboration is to improve the quality and accuracy of information provided
57:to all involved in a student’s transition into higher education, as well as to reduce the reporting burden on data
57:providers.
57:The CDS is a set of standards and definitions of data items rather than a survey instrument or set of data
57:represented in a database. Each of the higher education surveys conducted by the participating publishers
57:incorporates items from the CDS as well as unique items proprietary to each publisher. Consequently, the
57:publishers’ surveys differ in that they utilize varying numbers of items from the CDS.
57:Those who report data for their colleges are urged to abide by the definitions and the cohorts specified when
57:answering CDS items. They are also urged to use the answers to CDS items when responding to the
57:numerous survey requests they receive, by distributing photocopies of their answers, posting them on their
57:websites, or by other effective means.
57:This Excel template is designed for CSV export. On each section tab, responses are stored in columns | 505:AA–
57:AL | 70:. Before exporting, verify that your entries appear correctly on the | 355:Answer Sheet | 420:. The Answer Sheet
57:includes field mappings to the | 189:U.S. News fillable PDF | 295:and to the | 342:Word | 370:version. Please confirm with the
57:receiving organization that it is able to accept a CSV of your responses. For more information about the
57:Common Data Set (CDS) initiative, visit | 231:https://commondataset.org/
57:Please contact the | 141:College Board College Survey | 283:team at collegesurvey@collegeboard.org should you have
57:any questions about this Excel template.
57:*Note: At the time of publication, not all questions on the | 304:Excel | 332:template can be matched to a | 464:U.S. News
57:fillable PDF | 114:equivalent. The matching cells for these questions are in light red on the Answer Sheet. Please
57:confirm all questions, responses, and mapping before submitting a CSV to another organization.
57:*Note: As of publication, some questions in the | 263:Word | 291:template do not map directly—or require different
57:formatting—to an equivalent field in the | 230:U.S. News fillable PDF | 336:and in the | 382:Excel | 411:template. An example are
57:those questions with multiple category options such as "Very Important, Important, Considered, Not
57:Considered". The matching cells for these questions may appear as duplicates (light red on the Answer Sheet)
57:or a single category is listed as the code value (c7_state_residency_very_important ). Please review and verify
