State Profile - Virginia

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering; however, common course numbering exists at the community college level. The State Council of Higher Education for Virginia has a course equivalency database.
Source
VCCS Policy 5.03
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
A Uniform Certificate of General Studies must be developed by the State Council of Higher Education, the Virginia Community College System and the public institutions. All credits earned in academic subject coursework by students attending a two-year college who complete the certificate must be transferable to a four-year public institution.
Source
Va. Code Ann. § 23-9.2:3.02
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
VCCS and Richard Bland College transfer students who earn an associate degree shall be considered to have met the lower division general education requirements of the senior institution.
Source
State Council of Higher Education for Virginia State Policy on College Transfer
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions. However, there is a reverse transfer toolkit that was assembled by a work group comprised of higher education stakeholders from various colleges.
