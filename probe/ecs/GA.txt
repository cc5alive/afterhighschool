Transfer and Articulation 2022

State Profile - Georgia

July 2022

TRANSFER AND ARTICULATION 2022
Statewide common course numbering
No
Description of statewide common course numbering
Does not have statewide common course numbering. However, the common course prefixes, numbers and descriptions are provided for all institutions in the University System of Georgia. They do not apply to the Technical College System of Georgia.
Source
USG Board Policy 2.4.10
Transferable core of lower- division courses
Yes
Description of transferable core of lower-division courses
Core curriculum is transferable to all University System of Georgia institutions and Technical College System of Georgia.
Source
USG Board Policy 3.3.1 and 3.3.5.2
Statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Yes
Description of statewide guaranteed transfer of an associate degree
Associate of arts and associate of science degrees are primarily intended to be transfer degrees leading to a bachelor degree. All associate of science degrees offered by the TCSG institutions must have articulation agreements with the USG.
Source
Board Policy 3.8.2 and 3.3.5.1
Statewide reverse transfer
No
Description of statewide reverse transfer
Reverse transfer is done at individual institutions.
